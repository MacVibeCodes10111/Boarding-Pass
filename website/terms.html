An account is optional and only moves your careers between your devices. To create one:

You can keep up to 4 careers in the cloud and choose which. Cloud copies are there to move careers, not to back them up: each one is deleted once your other devices have it, or after 90 days, and accounts unused for 12 months are deleted, as the Privacy policy explains. When the same career is played on two devices before they sync, one version may be replaced or kept as a separate career, depending on your version of the game, so don't count on both surviving. Your flights appear on each player's own game clock, so what they see won't match the time in your game. An alliance can be found by any player who uses alliances: they see its name, tag, colours, logo, description, when it started, whether it's open or asks airlines to request to join, and, for each airline in it, the airline's name, code, callsign, colours, logo and summary, ranked against the others. They don't see its airlines' flights unless they're friends or in the alliance.
