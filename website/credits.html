How it's made

Boarding Pass is designed and directed by Mac and developed with the help of AI: much of its code and text is written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, then reviewed and tested before each release.

Data

Airports, runways and navaids: OurAirports, public domain. The world map on the home page is drawn from the same airports.

Airline routes flown today: airline-route-data by Jonty Wareing, gathered from airlines' published routes. Route demand and the other airlines' schedules in the game are worked out from these routes.

Population around airports: GeoNames, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0).
