Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Short version: Boarding Pass has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. Accounts are optional and only for cloud saves. If you don't sign in, your airline lives on your own device and is never sent to us. If you do, we keep only what cloud saves need, for as short a time as we can. We don't sell or share anyone's personal information.

What this covers

This policy covers the website at boardingpass.my, the game in the browser at play.boardingpass.my (and the test version at test.boardingpass.my), the Boarding Pass desktop apps for Mac and Windows, and the optional Boarding Pass account and cloud saves. In it, “we” and “us” means the developer, Mac, who is responsible for them (the “controller” under EU and UK law).

Without an account

We collect nothing. The game and this site send nothing about you or your games to us. We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, session recording or any similar tools, and we don't buy or receive information about you from anyone else. The only personal information that reaches anyone is your IP address and ordinary request details, which every website you visit receives, as described under Services that handle your requests. If you message us on Discord or GitHub, we see what you send there, and use it only to reply and to improve the game.

With an account (cloud saves)

Signing in is optional, for players 13 and over, and is only used to move your careers between your devices. When you have an account, we keep:

  • Your account: a random account number, and either the username you chose (your password is stored only as a secure hash, and we can't see it) or, if you sign in with Google or Discord, the email address, name and profile picture link they send us. Discord also sends your Discord user name. We use these only to sign you in and to show you who you're signed in as. We never email you.
  • Your careers on their way between devices: a copy of a career you saved (the same scrambled and signed save the game keeps on your device), its airline name and when it was saved.
  • Your devices: a random number for each device you sign in on, its type (for example “Mac app” or “iPhone browser”) and when it last synced, so we know which devices still need a career.
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, and your IP address in our service provider's security logs. When you create a username account, a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address is kept for one day, only to stop anyone creating accounts in bulk.

Before you sign in, the game asks when you were born. It keeps only whether you're 13 or over, on your device, and never sends your birthday anywhere.

How long we keep it

  • A career copy is deleted as soon as all your other devices used in the last 30 days have it. A copy no other device has picked up is deleted after 90 days.
  • A device that hasn't synced for 180 days is forgotten. Signing out on a device forgets it at once.
  • Your account and everything with it are deleted when you delete it (Cloud saves → Delete account, in the game), or automatically after 12 months without use.
  • Our service provider's logs are kept only for a short time, set by the provider. Counts of how much each account has synced are kept for two days, to enforce fair-use limits.

What stays on your device

Everything else the game remembers is kept in your browser's storage (in the browser) or in the app's own data folder (in the desktop apps), on your device only:

  • Your careers: each saved airline (fleet, routes, schedule, finances), stored scrambled and signed so damage can be spotted.
  • Settings: map and display settings, colour theme, cabin layouts you saved, how panels are arranged, and whether you've seen the welcome tour.
  • If you sign in: your sign-in session, this device's random number, which careers it has synced, and the result of the age check.
  • Save files you export: written wherever you choose, on your device.
  • On this website: the airline name, code, hub and colours you choose in Print your boarding pass, a count of the times you pressed Board now (shown on the page), and, for the length of your visit, the latest version number and list of changes fetched from GitHub.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser (or play.boardingpass.my's, for careers in the browser), or remove the desktop app and its data. That deletes the careers on that device too, so use Menu → Export save file first if you want to keep one. The Cookies and storage page lists every item.

Your boarding pass link

When you press Board now or copy the link, the airline name, code, hub and colours you chose are put into the game's web address, so the game can fill them in. Like any web address, it ends up in your browser history and in the web host's logs for play.boardingpass.my, and anyone you send the link to can read it. Please don't put personal details in an airline name.

Services that handle your requests

These services receive your IP address and the details of each request (the address asked for, the time, and what your browser says about itself), and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Accounts and cloud saves: Supabase stores accounts and cloud saves for us, as our processor, on servers in the United States (Virginia), under a data processing agreement that includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google and Discord: only if you choose to sign in with them. They see that you signed in to Boarding Pass, under Google's privacy policy and Discord's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: boardingpass.my and play.boardingpass.my are hosted by Hostinger, whose servers keep standard access logs. We look at them only to fix problems or stop abuse. Hostinger's privacy policy.
  • Maps: map tiles, labels and map styles come from CARTO, and satellite imagery from EOX IT Services, when the map is shown. Their privacy policies are on their websites.
  • GitHub: this site asks GitHub for the latest version and the list of changes. The desktop apps ask GitHub for updates when they start, and are downloaded from GitHub. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Discord and GitHub, if you follow a link to them, under their own policies.

Everything else the game and this site use, including the map engine and the typefaces, is served by our own host. Requests to CARTO, EOX and GitHub are sent without cookies, so those services can't use cookies to recognise you. These services may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries.

Why this is lawful (EU and UK players)

  • Accounts and cloud saves: to provide the service you asked for when you created an account (GDPR and UK GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Security logs, host logs and messages you send us: our legitimate interest in keeping the game and sites working and safe, and in answering you (Article 6(1)(f)).

Storage on your device is used only for features you ask for, as explained on the Cookies and storage page. We make no automated decisions about you and don't profile anyone.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, including those in the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other US state privacy laws, and Canada's PIPEDA.

  • Delete: delete your account at any time in the game (Cloud saves → Delete account). It removes the account and every cloud copy at once.
  • Copy: your careers are on your devices, and Menu → Export save file gives you a copy. For a copy of your account details, ask us.
  • Anything else: ask us (see Contact). We'll answer within 30 days, and we'll never treat you differently for asking.
  • Sale and sharing: we don't sell personal information, don't share it for targeted advertising, and don't profile anyone. There is nothing to opt out of, and we treat Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as a request not to be tracked, which we already honour.
  • Sensitive information: we don't collect any.
  • Complaints: if you are in the EU or UK, you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Boarding Pass is suitable for all ages, and nobody needs an account or any personal information to play. Accounts and cloud saves are only for players 13 and over: the game asks for a birth month and year first, and doesn't offer sign-in to younger players. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (as covered by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). If you think a child under 13 has an account or has sent us personal information, tell us and we'll delete it. Players under 18 should check with a parent or guardian before creating an account. The Discord server is for people old enough to use Discord under its own terms.

Security

  • The websites and the cloud service are reached only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Accounts and cloud copies are stored encrypted by our provider. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. The game can reach only your own account's data, and only through a few fixed actions (upload, download, delete).
  • Accounts never use email: there are no sign-in links, password-reset or address-change emails, which also means nobody can create an account in your email address's name.
  • Syncing and account creation have fair-use limits, and the browser game only runs its own code and only talks to the services listed above.
  • Cloud copies of careers are scrambled and signed like the saves on your device, but they are not end-to-end encrypted, so don't put personal details in airline names.
  • The desktop apps only install updates signed with the developer's private key, and only run code that comes with them.
  • If a breach ever affected your account, we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

No system is perfectly secure, so we keep as little as we can, for as short a time as we can.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and its date before the change, and say so in What's new. We won't start collecting more personal information without asking you first.

Contact

Boarding Pass is made by one developer, Mac, with the help of AI (Claude, by Anthropic). Ask anything on the Boarding Pass Discord or in a GitHub issue. For anything private (a privacy request, for example), send the developer a direct message on that Discord server rather than posting in a public channel.
