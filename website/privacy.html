Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Short version: Boarding Pass has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. Accounts are optional, and are for cloud saves and sharing with other players. If you don't sign in, your airline lives on your own device and is never sent to us. If you do, we keep only what those features need, for as short a time as we can. We don't sell or share anyone's personal information.

What this covers

This policy covers the website at boardingpass.my, the game in the browser at play.boardingpass.my (and the test version at test.boardingpass.my), the Boarding Pass desktop apps for Mac and Windows, and the optional Boarding Pass account, cloud saves and sharing. In it, “we” and “us” means the developer, Mac, who is responsible for them (the “controller” under EU and UK law).

Without an account

We collect nothing. The game and this site send nothing about you or your games to us. We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, session recording or any similar tools, and we don't buy or receive information about you from anyone else. The only personal information that reaches anyone is your IP address and ordinary request details, which every website you visit receives, as described under Services that handle your requests. If you message us by email, Discord or GitHub, we see what you send, and your email address if you use email, and use it only to reply, deal with your request and improve the game.

With an account (cloud saves)

Signing in is optional, for players 13 and over. It moves your careers between your devices and, only if you turn it on, lets you share with other players (see below). When you have an account, we keep:

  • Your account: a random account number, and either the username you chose (your password is stored only as a secure hash, and we can't see it) or, if you sign in with Google or Discord, the email address, name and profile picture link they send us. Discord also sends your Discord user name. We use these only to sign you in and to show you who you're signed in as. We don't send you email, unless you write to us first or we have to tell you about a security problem with your account.
  • Your careers on their way between devices: for up to 4 careers you choose to keep in the cloud, a copy of the career (the same scrambled and signed save the game keeps on your device), its airline name and when it was saved, and which careers you chose.
  • Your devices: a random number for each device you sign in on, its type (for example “Mac app” or “iPhone browser”) and when it last synced, so we know which devices still need a career.
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, and your IP address in our service provider's security logs. When you create a username account, scrambled (hashed) forms of your IP address and of this device's random number are kept for one day, only to limit how many accounts can be created (20 a day per connection and per device).

Before you sign in, the game asks when you were born. It keeps only whether you're 13 or over, on your device, and never sends your birthday anywhere.

When you share with other players

Sharing your airline and logos, friends, showing your flights to friends, alliances and the marketplace are being tried out on the test version (test.boardingpass.my and the test apps). Sharing needs an account and is off until you turn it on.

  • What we keep: for each career you choose to share (up to 10): your airline's name, code, callsign, colours, logos, liveries, a summary such as fleet size and routes, and whether it plays Career or Sandbox, a random number other players will know it by, its friend code, its friends and requests, and its alliance: the alliance's name, tag, colours, logo and description, its airlines and who leads it, its invites, whether it's open or asks airlines to request to join, the requests to join it (an unanswered request goes after 30 days), and a history of who started, changed, joined, left, led or was removed from it (kept for 12 months). Also, for the marketplace: each listing (the aircraft's type, registration, seats and the price you ask), who bought it, and when. Also, for your account: the players you've blocked (with the airline's name at the time you blocked it, so you can tell who to unblock), how many friend requests and alliance changes you've tried today, and your marketplace listings and trades.
  • Your flights, if you choose to show them: if you turn on “Show my aircraft on friends' maps”, we keep your shared career's weekly schedule on Cloudflare (R2 storage): routes, flight numbers, departure and flight times, days, aircraft types, registrations and which livery each aircraft wears. That airline's friends' games, and the games of the other airlines in its alliance, use it to show its aircraft. It has no money, passengers or other details of your career. Turning this off, stopping sharing or deleting your account stops friends seeing it straight away, and removes the copy we keep within a day. A friend's game that's already open can keep showing your aircraft for up to 10 minutes.
  • Who sees it: that airline's friends. Each airline you share has its own friends. Career and Sandbox airlines are kept apart: they can't be friends or be in the same alliance, and the same goes for the marketplace. When you ask someone to be friends, they see your airline's name, code, callsign, colours, logo and summary, so they can tell who's asking. You see theirs only once they accept. A request nobody answers goes after 30 days, and one that's declined is kept for 7 days so the same player can't ask again straight away. The airlines in an alliance see each other like friends do (including flights, for those that show them), Any player who uses alliances can find an alliance by searching or among the few picked at random each week, and sees its name, tag, colours, logo, description, when it started, whether it's open, and its airlines' names, codes, callsigns, colours, logos and summaries, but not their flights. An airline that asks to join is seen by that alliance's leader, like a friend request. Only a leader's friends can be invited; an invite nobody answers goes after 30 days. Marketplace listings are seen by every player who uses the marketplace, with the selling airline's name, code, colours, logo and alliance tag; once something sells, the buyer and seller see each other's airline name. Other players never see your email address, your sign-in details or your account number. Friends are found by an airline's friend code, never by username.
  • Uploaded logos: stored on Cloudflare (R2 storage), named by a fingerprint of the picture, so the same picture is stored once. We keep a record of which shared airlines use each one and when they last did, its size and, for two days, when each account uploaded, to enforce the limits and remove a logo if it's reported.
  • Moderation: names and text are checked automatically. The developer or a trusted moderator looks at each uploaded logo before other players can see it, and at reported content, only to stop abuse.
  • Reports and moderation records: a report keeps who reported what, and why, and a copy of what was reported as it was then. A moderation record keeps what was done, to which account, by whom, when and why. Each is deleted after 12 months at most.
  • Refused logos: we keep a refused logo's fingerprint (a code worked out from the picture, not the picture) so the same picture can't be uploaded again. Its file is deleted, and the fingerprint isn't linked to you once you stop using that logo.
  • Marketplace records: a record of each trade (the players, the item, the in-game price and when) is kept for 12 months, to deal with cheating and reverse trades if needed.
  • Deleting it: stop sharing an airline and its details, friend code and friends are deleted at once, and your logos usually are too (otherwise by the next night). An approved logo can stay in other players' browsers for up to a day. Deleting your account, or having it deleted after 12 months without use, deletes all of this within a day, except for three things: reports and moderation records involving you stay until their 12 months are up, because they're needed to deal with abuse; records of trades other players took part in keep a placeholder instead of your airline; and refused logos' fingerprints stay, linked to nobody.
  • Why this is lawful: to provide the features you choose to use (Article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in keeping them safe and fair (Article 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

  • A career copy is deleted as soon as all your other devices used in the last 30 days have it. A copy no other device has picked up is deleted after 90 days.
  • A device that hasn't synced for 180 days is forgotten. Signing out on a device forgets it at once.
  • Your account and everything with it are deleted when you delete it (Cloud saves → Delete account, in the game), or automatically after 12 months without use.
  • Your shared airline stays until you stop sharing or your account is deleted. Either takes it out of its alliance straight away; an alliance with no airlines left is deleted. An alliance's history is deleted after 12 months. Reports, moderation records and marketplace records are deleted after 12 months at most.
  • A notice telling you a moderator changed something of yours is kept for 90 days.
  • Emails and messages you send us are kept for as long as we need them to deal with what you asked, and for at most 12 months after that unless a legal complaint needs them for longer.
  • Our service provider's logs are kept only for a short time, set by the provider. Counts of how much each account has synced are kept for two days, to enforce fair-use limits.

What stays on your device

Everything else the game remembers is kept in your browser's storage (in the browser) or in the app's own data folder (in the desktop apps), on your device only:

  • Your careers: each saved airline (fleet, routes, schedule, finances), stored scrambled and signed so damage can be spotted.
  • Settings: map and display settings, colour theme, cabin layouts you saved, how panels are arranged, and whether you've seen the welcome tour.
  • If you sign in: your sign-in session, this device's random number, which careers it has synced, whether careers download by themselves, what you've chosen to share, and the result of the age check.
  • On this website: the airline name, code, hub and colours you choose in Print your boarding pass, a count of the times you pressed Board now (shown on the page), and, for the length of your visit, the latest version number and list of changes fetched from GitHub.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser (or play.boardingpass.my's, for careers in the browser), or remove the desktop app and its data. That deletes the careers on that device too, apart from any kept with cloud saves. The Cookies and storage page lists every item.

Your boarding pass link

When you press Board now or copy the link, the airline name, code, hub and colours you chose are put into the game's web address, so the game can fill them in. Like any web address, it ends up in your browser history and in the web host's logs for play.boardingpass.my, and anyone you send the link to can read it. Please don't put personal details in an airline name.

Services that handle your requests

These services receive your IP address and the details of each request (the address asked for, the time, and what your browser says about itself), and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • Accounts, cloud saves and shared airlines: Supabase stores accounts, cloud saves and shared airline details, including liveries, for us, as our processor, on servers in the United States (Virginia), under a data processing agreement that includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Shared logos: Cloudflare stores uploaded logos for us, as our processor, and serves approved ones to players. It only comes into play if you or a player you share with uses these features. Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google and Discord: only if you choose to sign in with them. They see that you signed in to Boarding Pass, under Google's privacy policy and Discord's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: boardingpass.my, play.boardingpass.my and test.boardingpass.my are hosted by Hostinger, whose servers keep standard access logs. We look at them only to fix problems or stop abuse. Hostinger's privacy policy.
  • Maps: map tiles, labels and map styles come from CARTO, and satellite imagery from EOX IT Services, when the map is shown. Their privacy policies are on their websites.
  • GitHub: this site asks GitHub for the latest version and the list of changes. The desktop apps ask GitHub for updates when they start, and are downloaded from GitHub. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Discord and GitHub, if you follow a link to them, under their own policies.
  • Email: if you email us, your message is handled by Google (Gmail), under Google's privacy policy.

Everything else the game and this site use, including the map engine and the typefaces, is served by our own host. Requests to CARTO, EOX, GitHub and our logo service are sent without cookies, so those services can't use cookies to recognise you. These services may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries.

Why this is lawful (EU and UK players)

  • Accounts, cloud saves and sharing: to provide the service you asked for when you created an account or turned sharing on (GDPR and UK GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Security logs, host logs, reports, moderation records and messages you send us: our legitimate interest in keeping the game and sites working, safe and fair, and in answering you (Article 6(1)(f)).

The automatic name check runs on your device, and again on the server for anything you share, and decides nothing about you except whether a name can be used. Storage on your device is used only for features you ask for, as explained on the Cookies and storage page. We make no automated decisions about you and don't profile anyone.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, including those in the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and other US state privacy laws, and Canada's PIPEDA.

  • Delete: delete your account at any time in the game (Cloud saves → Delete account). It removes the account and every cloud copy at once, and your shared logos within a day. The few records kept after that are listed under “When you share with other players”.
  • Copy: your careers are on your devices, and cloud saves bring them to any device you sign in on. For a copy of your account details or anything else we hold, ask us.
  • Anything else: ask us (see Contact). We'll answer within 30 days, and we'll never treat you differently for asking. If we can't do what you ask, for example because a record is still needed to deal with abuse, we'll tell you why.
  • Sale and sharing: we don't sell personal information, don't share it for targeted advertising, and don't profile anyone. There is nothing to opt out of, and we treat Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as a request not to be tracked, which we already honour.
  • Sensitive information: we don't collect any.
  • Complaints: if you are in the EU or UK, you can complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Boarding Pass is suitable for all ages, and nobody needs an account or any personal information to play. Accounts, cloud saves and sharing are only for players 13 and over: the game asks for a birth month and year first, and doesn't offer sign-in to younger players. Some countries set a higher age for agreeing to online services without a parent's permission. If yours does and you're under it, get a parent or guardian's permission first. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (as covered by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). If you think a child under 13 has an account or has sent us personal information, tell us and we'll delete it. Players under 18 should check with a parent or guardian before creating an account. The Discord server is for people old enough to use Discord under its own terms.

Security

  • The websites and the cloud service are reached only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Accounts and cloud copies are stored encrypted by our provider. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. The game can reach only your own account's data, and only through a few fixed actions (such as upload, download, delete and share).
  • Accounts never use email: there are no sign-in links, password-reset or address-change emails, which also means nobody can create an account in your email address's name.
  • Syncing and account creation have fair-use limits, and the browser game only runs its own code and only talks to the services listed above.
  • Cloud copies of careers are scrambled and signed like the saves on your device, but they are not end-to-end encrypted, so don't put personal details in airline names.
  • The desktop apps only install updates signed with the developer's private key, and only run code that comes with them.
  • If a breach ever affected your account, we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires. Most accounts have no email address, so we'd tell players on this site, in What's new and on the Discord.

No system is perfectly secure, so we keep as little as we can, for as short a time as we can.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and its date before the change, and say so in What's new. We won't start collecting more personal information without asking you first.
