Privacy

Short version: Boarding Pass has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your airline lives on your own device.

Your careers

In the browser, the game saves your careers and settings in your browser's own storage on your device. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browsing data deletes them, so use Menu → Export save file to keep a copy or move a career to another device. The desktop apps keep their saves on your computer.

What other services see

Like any website, the services the game and this site load from see your IP address and the request:

  • Maps: CARTO (map tiles and labels) and EOX (satellite imagery), when the map is shown.
  • GitHub: this site asks GitHub for the latest version and the list of changes. The desktop apps ask GitHub for updates when they start.
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces on this site.
  • Hosting: our web host keeps standard server logs.
  • Discord and GitHub, if you follow a link to them, under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

Neither the game nor this site sets cookies for tracking or advertising.
