Cookies and storage

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Short version: neither the game nor this website sets any cookies. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, pixels or fingerprinting. The game keeps your careers and settings in your browser's storage on your device, only so it works. Nothing in it leaves your device, except your careers if you sign in to cloud saves.

Cookies

We don't set cookies on boardingpass.my, play.boardingpass.my or in the desktop apps. The map, update and version requests the game and site make to other services (CARTO, EOX and GitHub) are sent without cookies, so those services can't use them to recognise you. Our web host, Hostinger, may use strictly necessary technical cookies to protect its servers against attacks and keep them running; they are not used by us, or for tracking or advertising. If you choose to sign in with Google or Discord, their own sign-in pages use their own cookies, under their policies.

Storage on your device

Instead of cookies, the game and this site use your browser's local storage (and, for the version list, session storage). In the desktop apps the same items live in the app's own data folder. Nothing in them is sent to us or anyone else, except that cloud saves (only if you sign in) send your careers and your sign-in session to our cloud service. This is everything we store:

Why there is no cookie banner

The EU ePrivacy Directive and the UK's PECR require consent before storing information on your device, except where it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Everything above is only there to remember your own games and choices, or to keep you signed in when you chose to sign in, so it falls under that exception, and there is nothing to consent to. If we ever add anything that isn't strictly necessary, we'll ask first.

Removing it

Clear this site's data (or play.boardingpass.my's) in your browser's settings, or remove the desktop app and its data. Clearing deletes your careers, so use Menu → Export save file first to keep them. You can also block site data in your browser; the game then still runs, but can't keep your careers between visits.
