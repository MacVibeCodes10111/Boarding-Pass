What you'll be doing

A tycoon game with a flight tracker built in. The money side is a real business to run; the map side is the reward for running it well.

Start at a real airport

3,244 airports with scheduled service, each with its real runways, sized by the largest aircraft they take. Buy or lease from 98 aircraft types and fit out the cabin.

Build the schedule

Pick routes, departure times, days and fares. Demand depends on the cities, the distance and what you charge; the week view shows every aircraft's day at a glance.

Watch every flight

Your aircraft push back, taxi on the airport's real taxiways, take off from the runway the wind favours and land with the weather. Tilt into 3D to see them at cruising altitude.

Share the sky

Up to 10,000 other aircraft fly real airline routes around you, queue at the same runways and fight you for gates.

Keep the books

Weekly profit and cash flow, loans you can pay down a bit at a time, a bank that rewards milestones, and bankruptcy if you overreach.

Play anywhere

In the browser on a phone, tablet or computer, or as an app for Mac and Windows that keeps itself up to date. Add it to your home screen to play full screen.
